Dr. Mulat Biography & Accreditations

Dr. Mulat completed his Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) with honors, followed by advanced residency training in Orthodontics and Cosmetic Implantology. With over 15 years of surgical excellence in Addis Ababa, he has treated thousands of complex alignment cases and dental restoration procedures.

He is a member of the Ethiopian Dental Association (EDA) and actively participates in international continuous orthodontic education forums across Africa and Europe.
